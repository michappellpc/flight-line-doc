# Flight Line Doc

A small pixel-art life-sim for the browser. You're the new flight surgeon at an Army airfield: see sick call, make fitness-for-duty calls on aircrew, manage your energy, supplies and money, befriend the base crew, and earn the trust of your aircrews.

**Play:** https://michappellpc.github.io/flight-line-doc/

## Design
See [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) for the game design: pillars, systems, story, content targets and the medical accuracy policy.

## Controls
- Move: WASD or arrow keys (on-screen d-pad on touch devices)
- Interact: E, Space, or the on-screen E button
- Progress saves in your browser when you sleep

## Notes
- Single file, no build step: open `index.html` in a browser.
- All cases, art and text are original and fictional. This is a game for fun and learning, **not medical advice**.
- Not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Army, the Department of Defense, or any other game or publisher.
